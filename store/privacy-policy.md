# BrightTube Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Last updated: 2026-10-06

## English
BrightTube does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data.

The only information the extension saves is your chosen brightness level (a number from 0 to 100). It is stored using Chrome's built-in storage (`chrome.storage.sync`), which Chrome may sync across your own signed-in browsers. This data never leaves Chrome's storage and is never sent to the developer or any third party.

BrightTube runs only on youtube.com, where it changes the brightness of the video element and displays a brightness selector. It does not read your browsing history, account information, or video content, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

BrightTube is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google.

## 繁體中文

BrightTube 只在 youtube.com 上運作，用來改變影片的亮度並顯示亮度選單。它不會讀取你的瀏覽紀錄、帳號資訊或影片內容，也不含任何分析、廣告或追蹤程式。

BrightTube 與 YouTube 或 Google 無關，也未獲其認可或贊助。
